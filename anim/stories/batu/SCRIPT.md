# Batu's winter invasion of Rus (1237-38)

## Facts and sources (Wikipedia: "Mongol invasion of Kievan Rus'", "Siege of Ryazan", "Battle of Kolomna", "Siege of Vladimir", "Ignach Cross", "Novgorod Republic")
- Batu, grandson of Genghis Khan, led the western campaign; army size estimates range from ~35,000 to 140,000 ("tens of thousands").
- By 1237 the Mongol Empire held northern China (Jin fell 1234), Central Asia (Khwarazm 1219-21) up to the Caspian.
- Rus was split into rival principalities; Grand Prince Yuri II of Vladimir refused to help Ryazan.
- Ryazan fell 21 Dec 1237; Kolomna and Moscow Jan 1238; Vladimir 7 Feb 1238; Torzhok 5 Mar 1238.
- The Mongols used frozen rivers as roads in winter (Oka, Klyazma, Tvertsa).
- In March 1238 they turned back at Ignach Cross, ~100 km from Novgorod; the spring thaw (rasputitsa) is the usual explanation.
- Novgorod was never sacked by the Mongols; it paid tribute to the Golden Horde from 1259 until 1478.
