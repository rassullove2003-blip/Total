# Channel rules (working title TBD)

Read before writing any script.

## Identity
- English, US/UK audience. History explained on animated 2D maps with cartoon historical figures (our own character style).
- Niche: Eurasia beyond Western Europe — steppe empires (Mongols, Golden Horde, Huns, Turks, Timur), Silk Road cities and caravans, Rus/Russia, Central Asia, Persia, China borderlands. Western Europe only when it collides with these.
- No host character. Narrator voice only (Kokoro bm_george, British).
- Every story = a surprising true paradox with a clear cause → effect chain that can be SHOWN on a map.

## Script formula
Hook (paradox as fact, 10-16 words, brutal wording) → "start from the beginning" setting with year → cause chain 3-6 beats on the map → mystery "Wait...?" → "You see," the real mechanism → ironic payoff → OPEN FORK ending (question, not a full stop). ~3.7-4 words/s, 60-100 s. See notes/learned.md.

## Comment engine (every video)
- End on a debate: two sides, both plausible.
- Or a personal dilemma: "Would you have…?"
- Mild rivalry: Mongols vs Rus, East vs West, "who really won".
- Hidden detail in every video (e.g. a tiny camel somewhere on the map): "Did you spot it?" → rewatches.
- Loop: last line flows into the first line.
- Pinned comment: our A/B question.
- Provocative framing is fine, but every fact must be true and sourced (sources at top of SCRIPT.md).

## Hard lines (they kill the channel, not just "rules")
- No others' footage/music/characters (copyright strikes → channel deleted).
- No invented facts (one debunk comment at the top kills trust).
- No current politics/war (no ad revenue).
