# Channel rules — Lightbulb Moments (working title)

Read before writing any script.

## Identity
- English, US/UK audience. Host: Bulb the scientist (B4: glasses, lab coat, screw-base neck, filament hair).
- Signature: filament lights up on "You see," (the explanation beat).
- Host personality: sharp, sarcastic genius. Dark humour, strong opinions, mocks human stupidity in the story. Never neutral, never boring.
- Topics: true stories that sound absurd, explained by simple science. Pillars: physics disasters, why things are built that way, accidental inventions, crazy rules with logic. ~1/3 Russia/USSR stories (Tunguska, Kola borehole, Oymyakon, Tsar Bomba, Dyatlov Pass, Karachay).

## Script formula
Hook (paradox as fact, 10-16 words, brutal wording) → setting (1-2 lines) → escalation 3-6 beats → mystery "Wait...?" → "You see," + everyday analogy → ironic payoff → OPEN FORK ending (question, not a full stop). See notes/learned.md.

## Comment engine (every video)
- End on a debate: two sides, both plausible ("the cold or the steel?").
- Or a personal dilemma: "Would you have…?"
- Mild rivalry: USSR vs USA, genius vs idiot, "who was to blame".
- Unsolved mysteries: let viewers post their theory.
- Hidden mini lightbulb in every video: "Did you spot it?" → rewatches.
- Loop: last line flows into the first line.
- Pinned comment: our A/B question.
- Provocative framing is fine, but every fact must be true and sourced (sources at top of SCRIPT.md).

## Hard lines (they kill the channel, not just "rules")
- No others' footage/music/characters (copyright strikes → channel deleted).
- No invented facts (one debunk comment at the top kills trust; misinformation labels).
- No current politics/war (no ad revenue).
